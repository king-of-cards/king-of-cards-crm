# Planned CRM Beta Modules

The first backend milestone is infrastructure only. The following modules describe the intended development beta and are not implemented yet. Use synthetic customer and lead records exclusively.

- **Customer master:** shared customer records; deduplicate by mobile number and service.
- **Lead intake:** sources include online ads and showroom walk-ins, with service-specific lead records.
- **Vendor assignment:** assign leads to vendors and enforce vendor-scoped access.
- **Lead lifecycle:** track stages and schedule follow-ups.
- **Referrals and coupons:** capture referral attribution and coupon usage.
- **Commissions:** record commission attribution and status.
- **Dashboards:** present role-based views for CRM and vendor roles.
